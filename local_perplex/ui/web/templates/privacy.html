Where your data goes

This page reflects your current settings, so it changes when you switch models or search providers.

  • Your questions and answers {% if status.local %}Written by {{ current_model }} through {{ status.provider_label }} on this device. Nothing is sent to an AI company. {% else %}Sent to {{ status.provider_label }} to generate the answer, together with the retrieved passages. Their data policy applies. Switch to a local runtime in Models & search to keep this on your device.{% endif %}
  • Web search A short search query, not your full conversation, goes to {{ search_label }}. Result pages are then fetched directly by this device with TLS verification on. Questions answered from the model's own knowledge make no web requests at all.
  • Your documents Parsed and indexed on this device. Only the few passages relevant to a question are included in the prompt.
  • Library, conversations and knowledge graph Stored as JSON files and a SQLite database in the project folder. No account, no telemetry, no analytics, and no third-party scripts or fonts in this interface.
  • Incognito questions Not written to the library, the sidebar or the knowledge graph.
  • The server Listens on 127.0.0.1 by default, and rejects requests from other websites so a page in another tab can't change your settings or read your keys.
